Road to Quebec!

Pee-Wee Quebec International Hockey Tournament

February 10–21, 2027 • Quebec City, Canada

The St. Louis AAA Blues 2014 team is planning to compete at the prestigious 2027 Pee-Wee Quebec International Hockey Tournament — one of the most celebrated youth hockey events in the world. To make this once-in-a-lifetime experience possible, we are seeking community partners who share our commitment to youth athletics, teamwork, and excellence. Your contribution directly supports our players' journey to Quebec City.
