Road to Quebec!

Pee-Wee Quebec International Hockey Tournament

February 10–21, 2027 • Quebec City, Canada

The 2014 AAA St Louis Blues team is working toward earning a spot at the prestigious 2027 Pee-Wee Quebec International Hockey Tournament — one of the most celebrated youth hockey events in the world. To help make this once-in-a-lifetime opportunity possible, we are seeking community partners who share our commitment to youth athletics, teamwork, and excellence. Your contribution directly supports our players' journey toward Quebec City.
