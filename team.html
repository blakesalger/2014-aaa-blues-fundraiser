The Team

Meet the players, coaches, and staff behind the 2014 AAA St Louis Blues.

A Team Built for This Level

The 2014 AAA St. Louis Blues compete at the highest tier of youth travel hockey, and the schedule shows it. Season after season, this program tests itself against elite AAA competition from across the country — teams like Chicago Fury, Chicago Mission, Compuware, Little Caesars, Windy City, Indiana Elite, SouthEast Elite, Florida Alliance, and the Buffalo Jr. Sabres, along with showcase events like the TIER1 Elite Showcase in Detroit. That kind of national travel schedule isn't handed to just any team — it reflects the caliber of hockey this group has worked to build, game by game, season after season. It's also part of a much longer track record: the St. Louis AAA Blues pipeline has produced a long list of players who went on to play in the NHL, several of whom you'll meet as Honored Alumni at our upcoming Alumni Auction. Earning a place at the 2027 Pee-Wee Quebec International Hockey Tournament — the largest minor hockey tournament in the world, running continuously since 1960 — is itself a reflection of how far this group of players has come, and everyone involved is looking forward to seeing what they do with the opportunity.
