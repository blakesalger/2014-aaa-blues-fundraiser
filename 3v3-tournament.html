AAA Blues 3v3 Fundraiser Tournament

January 1–3, 2027 • Centene Community Ice Center

Gather your friends and teammates and join the 2014 AAA St. Louis Blues in raising money for our trip to the Pee-Wee Quebec International Hockey Tournament! This is a fun, relaxed 3v3 tournament for 6U and 8U players from around the St. Louis region. Bring your full team, a group of friends, or a mixed squad — this event is all about getting kids on the ice and having a great time. We'll do our best to match teams of similar skill level so every game is fun and competitive for everyone involved.

Why It Matters

The 2014 AAA Blues are working toward a spot at the 2027 Pee-Wee Quebec International Hockey Tournament — the world's largest minor hockey tournament, running since 1960, drawing teams from around the globe to Quebec City each February. Our organization has stood on this ice before: in 2009, the St. Louis Blues 1996 birth year team brought home the championship at this very event. Every 3v3 registration helps our 2014 team make that same trip.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is 3v3 hockey?

3v3 is a fast-paced, small-area game format with 3 skaters per side (plus a goalie), designed to maximize touches, ice time, and fun for young players. It's a great fit for the 6U/8U age groups.

Who can play?

Teams and players from the 6U and 8U age groups across the St. Louis region. You don't need a full team to register — gather a group of friends or teammates and sign up together.

Is this an international or competitive-level tournament?

No — this is a fun, relaxed local event hosted by the AAA Blues, not an international tournament. We'll do our best to match teams of similar skill level so every game is fun and competitive.

How many games will my team play?

Every team is guaranteed 8 games across the event.

What's the format?

Games are played in an 18-minute format.

Do we need to be available all three days?

Yes — but you'll only need to be available for your team's scheduled games. Playoff games will be held Sunday, January 3.

When will we know our schedule?

A formal game schedule will be released in December.

How much does it cost, and what's included?

Registration is $749 per team and includes 8 guaranteed games. Every registration also directly supports the 2014 AAA Blues' trip to the Pee-Wee Quebec International Hockey Tournament.

Is there a cap on the number of teams?

Yes — the field is capped at 40 teams to keep the schedule manageable and ensure every team gets their full 8 games. Register early, as spots will go fast.

Where is the event held?

Centene Community Ice Center, January 1–3, 2027.

What fun stuff is happening besides the games?

Louie the St. Louis Blues mascot will be stopping by, plus raffles (including mystery autographed pucks and a luxury purse raffle), 50/50, logo merchandise, and concessions. Every player gets a commemorative puck after their team's final playoff game.

Where does the money go?

All proceeds support the 2014 AAA St. Louis Blues' travel and participation in the 2027 Pee-Wee Quebec International Hockey Tournament. The St. Louis Blues Youth Hockey Association is a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit.

Thank you for being part of the journey — see you on the ice!
