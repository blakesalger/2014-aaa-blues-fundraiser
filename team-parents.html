Email & Text Templates

Use these templates when reaching out directly to a business owner you know.

Email
Hi [Owner/Manager Name], I'm [Your Name] — my son plays on the St. Louis AAA Blues 2014 hockey team. We're working toward getting our team to the 2027 Quebec International Tournament and are hosting a fundraising alumni auction on January 10, 2027 to help cover travel costs. We're looking for local businesses willing to donate an item or gift card for the auction. In exchange, your business would be featured in our printed event program and recognized at the event. Information on sponsorships is available at www.2014AAABlues.com. Would you be open to contributing? Even a small gift card makes a real difference. I'm happy to stop by at your convenience. Thanks so much, [Your Name] [Your Phone Number]
Text Message
Hi [Name], this is [Your Name] — my son plays on the St. Louis AAA Blues hockey team. We are working toward getting to the 2027 Quebec International Tournament and are putting together a fundraising alumni auction on January 10th. Would [Business Name] be open to donating a gift card or basket for the auction? Your business would be listed in our event program and recognized at the dinner. Happy to chat or stop by whenever is convenient! 🏒
