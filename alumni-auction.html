Alumni Auction

Dinner Auction & Fundraising Event — January 10, 2027 at Twisted Tree Banquet Center

Join us for an evening celebrating St. Louis Blues alumni and our 2014 AAA Blues players as we raise funds to send the team to the 2027 Pee-Wee Quebec International Hockey Tournament. The evening will feature a dinner, live and silent auction items, and appearances from St. Louis Blues alumni.
