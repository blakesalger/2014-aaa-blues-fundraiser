Pat Maroon

A proud St. Louis native, Pat Maroon became a local legend when he signed a one-year deal with his hometown Blues in 2018 and scored the double-overtime, series-clinching goal in Game 7 of the second round of the 2019 playoffs en route to the franchise's first-ever Stanley Cup. He famously lifted the Cup low enough for his young son to kiss it, a moment that captured the heart of the city. Maroon added two more championships with the Tampa Bay Lightning in 2020 and 2021, making him one of a handful of players in NHL history to win three consecutive Stanley Cups with two different teams.

Matthew Tkachuk

Though born in Scottsdale, Arizona, Matthew Tkachuk grew up in the St. Louis suburb of Chesterfield after his father, Keith Tkachuk, was traded to the Blues. The Calgary Flames selected him sixth overall in the 2016 NHL Draft, and he later became a driving force for the Florida Panthers following a blockbuster trade in 2022. Tkachuk has helped lead Florida to back-to-back Stanley Cup championships in 2024 and 2025, even bringing the Cup home to celebrate with the St. Louis community that raised him.

Luke Kunin

Born and raised in Chesterfield, Missouri, Luke Kunin got his start skating for the St. Louis AAA Blues and St. Louis Jr. Blues before starring at the University of Wisconsin. The Minnesota Wild made him a first-round pick, 15th overall, in the 2016 NHL Draft, and the center has since played in the NHL for the Wild, Nashville Predators, San Jose Sharks and Columbus Blue Jackets. He signed with the Florida Panthers organization for the 2025-26 season, continuing a pro career built on the same St. Louis ice where it began.
