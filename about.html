About the Tournament

The story behind Pee-Wee Quebec — and why this trip matters so much to our players and families.

Our Team's Journey

The 2014 AAA St Louis Blues birth year team is working toward earning a spot at the prestigious 2027 Pee-Wee Quebec International Hockey Tournament — one of the most celebrated youth hockey events in the world — taking place February 10–21, 2027 in Quebec City, Canada.

To help make this once-in-a-lifetime experience possible for our players and families, we are seeking community partners who share our commitment to youth athletics, teamwork, and excellence. Every contribution — whether through sponsorship, tournament registration, or the Alumni Auction — directly supports our players' journey to Quebec City.

About Pee-Wee Quebec

A brief history of the tournament our team is heading to.

Since 1960

The Quebec International Pee-Wee Hockey Tournament has run every year since 1960. The 2027 event will be its 67th edition.

The World's Largest

It holds the distinction of being the world's largest minor hockey tournament, drawing teams from numerous countries.

225,000+ Spectators

The 2026 edition alone drew over 225,000 spectators — a testament to the scale and community excitement around this event.

12 Days in Quebec City

The tournament runs roughly 12 days across multiple arenas, combining elite competition with cultural exchange, billeting, and team travel experiences.

Learn more directly from the tournament organizers at tournoipee-wee.qc.ca.
